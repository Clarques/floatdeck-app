Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how FloatDeck ("we", "our", or "the Extension") handles user data. Our core philosophy is "Privacy by Design".

1. Data Handling Transparency
FloatDeck is a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or user-generated content to any external servers. All data processing occurs strictly within your local browser environment.

2. Use of Permissions (Google Limited Use Policy)
In compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, we declare the use of the following permissions:

  • Tabs & Windows: Used exclusively to facilitate the "float" functionality, allowing the user to detach and reposition browser tabs as independent windows.
  • Storage: Used to save user-defined layout presets and App Library shortcuts locally on the device.

FloatDeck's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Third-Party Access
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is no database of user information to share.

4. Children's Privacy
Our extension does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect identifiable information from children.
